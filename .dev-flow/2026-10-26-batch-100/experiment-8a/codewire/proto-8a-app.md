finding C4 no deferred load step resets the change document; the safe reopen point is before the install

seen: read-only trace at dc3b314. The only document reset is _reset_project_session (:10503, rebind :10313, clear_paste_buffer and mark_loaded :10339-10340). A transitive scan of the 51 S19TuiApp methods reachable from the deferred steps (update_sections, update_hex_view, update_alt_hex_view, update_mac_hex_view, update_a2l_view, update_project_labels, update_memory_map, _refresh_loaded_panel, _rehome_focus_after_load, watch_current_file) found 0 lines with "_change_service =", "ChangeService(", "mark_loaded", "mark_dirty", ".mark_saved", "refresh_entries", "clear_paste_buffer", ".load(", "load_text" or ".document =". cause: the deferred chain renders views from current_file; it never writes the change service or the panel flag.
effect: a reopen anywhere after :10503 inside _handle_load_project survives the deferred steps.
decision: reopen between :10550 and :10551 (after the A2L parse at :10535 and the "nothing installable" return, before current_project is set and the install loop), with a2l_context = the dict :10535 returns. ### Design risks (seams and open points) — state after E3 round 2 (rulings in `../E3-rulings.md`)
R-1 (seam B, C3). The save passes the landing of an app-written document, which is already in its home, to gather_project_artifacts; the gather's class `in_home` (E3 R11) gives it no row and names it in GatherOutcome.active_document (project_gather_service.py:497-512). R-5 (E2 rule (b), the in-place rewrite). WITHDRAWN by E3 R1.
