### Checked and found sound (no finding)

- **Never-raises:** `read_recent_sources` and `record_recent_source` both have a last-resort guard. Their inner arms map every `OSError` to the specified problem word or to `False`. `_recent_entry_ok`'s check order means no later check can raise on what an earlier check let through: a NUL is refused before `PureWindowsPath`.
- **Ordering and de-dup:** the new entry goes first, then the existing accepted entries in stored order minus any whose `normcase` equals the new path's, cut to 20. A "could not be read" store is not written (LLR-172.1k). A "too large" or wrong-shape store is replaced by 1 entry, as LLR-172.1j specifies.
- **Byte cap:**
  - The reader makes one `read(65 537)`.
  - The writer drops the oldest entry until the payload fits, and gives `b""`, so no write, when one entry alone cannot fit.
  - The compact ASCII encoding matches LLR-172.1m's arithmetic: 20 × 3 240 characters fit; 3 245 characters keep 19 entries.
- **Staging:**
  - `mkstemp` in the store folder, then `os.replace`.
  - The staging file is unlinked in `finally` on every failure.
  - `mkdir(parents=False)` creates only `s19_app` (R7).
- **Typed route:** the absolute test runs on the stripped text (R2). No `resolve()` runs before the walk. The walk calls `lstat` on every prefix. The listing match returns the on-disk name (R3). `_handle_load_project` re-checks everything before the reset, so the classifier is not the only guard.
- **Tests:** the 141 nodes encode the requirement thresholds and their negative controls well. In particular, the `_OpenSpy` read-size and zero-open link arms, the 200-seed random-bytes arm, the AST census, and the junction, 8.3 and stream arms are discriminating. The conftest has a session guard for the real store plus an autouse per-test `LOCALAPPDATA`. That is correct and depends on seam S9 being honoured; it is.
- **The `test_b99_staging.py` allow-list re-pin:** justified. The store lives outside every work area, so outside LLR-155.3's scope, and no assertion was weakened.
