### The three acts (ISO 9241-210)

- **Automated walkthrough through the real mechanism:** `not performed` — P2 reviews requirements, and no surface ships yet. The prototype captures are painted results of the prototype, not of the shipped app.
- **Expert inspection against declared criteria:** `performed` — this review: the requirements' variant-independent clauses checked against the 5 surfaces' real captures at 118×54 and the round's risk table.
- **Evaluation with users:** `not performed` — the team is one person and this surface has no user outside it. The operator's prototype verdict is a design judgment, not a user evaluation.
