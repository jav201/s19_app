## BLUF

The design respects the ARQ boundaries. It moves no module, and every crossing of the frozen engine is named in the LLRs. The sequential plan is sound. §5 gives every TC and AT a group and declares the mutation that turns each group RED. The weak spot is the new group placement (B99-I2a). Its name and its consumers are declared, but its signature, its building increment, its partial-failure behaviour and its import edge are not. Its consumer list also names the headless engine `segment_ops`, which must not import `tui/workspace`. There is also a gap shared by B99-I2 and B99-I2a: a 0-byte payload can be "reused" onto another writer's 0-byte claim placeholder. Two smaller faults: the forward-applicability row for traceability points at an LLR column that §4 does not have, and the B99-I2 caller census misses one injected caller.
