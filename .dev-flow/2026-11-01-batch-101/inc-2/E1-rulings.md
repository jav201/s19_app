| D-1 | **QC-TM = executable guard for the margin; written rule for the clock.** A margin reporter (`report_margin(label, measured, bound) -> bound`) wraps the bound side of every upper timing bound: `assert took < report_margin("save-project", took, timing_bound(1.0))`. A static AST guard fails any upper timing bound that is not such a call (R-TST-040, 041). The clock-stop rule ("stop at a product stamp, never at a poll") is a convention plus a standing E5 step (R-TST-047). | The margin is checkable from the source: the bound side of the comparison is a fixed AST shape. Returning the bound keeps one source for the number and leaves each arm's own operator (`<` or `<=`) and its CI scaling untouched, so no arm gets stricter or looser. Where the clock stops is a judgement about the product (which stamp is the end of the requirement's interval); no static rule tells a product hook from a poll | a false positive or negative of the guard on a real arm at E4; then the guard's shape rule is narrowed with a cited case |
| D-2 | **QC-OR = executable ratchet guard + written rule.** The oracle helper already exists (`StatusLog`, unbounded, records every `set_status` message while it paints); no new helper.
