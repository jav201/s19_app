**In:**

Reproduced (C, census lines 9-42): of the 22 dialogs of the tree (every `ModalScreen` subclass), `VariantHelpScreen` (`screens.py:1091-1153`) is the only one with no product construction site; its text `VARIANT_HELP_TEXT` (`screens.py:1070-1088`) serves only it; one test node opens it by a direct push (`tests/test_tui_variants.py::test_variant_help_modal_fits_at_both_sizes`). - **F-34** (= **D6-12**, first half) — `action_view_main`, `action_view_mac` have no key and no palette entry; `action_view_alt` only an internal call. Reproduced (C lines 44-66; B lines 8-15): of the 83 `action_*` methods of the tree, only `S19TuiApp.action_view_main` and `action_view_mac` (`app.py:10753-10755`, `:10761-10763`) have no reach at all; `action_view_alt` (`:10757-10759`) is reached only by a call at `app.py:16840` inside `_jump_to_validation_issue_object`, whose one caller `_jump_to_validation_issue` has no product caller (C lines 67-70). None of the three is in `BINDINGS` or the palette (42 commands); keys `1`, `2`, `3` show Workspace, A2L Explorer and MAC View through `show_screen(...)` (B).
- **D3-7** (co-located with F-34, decision D-2) — `_jump_to_validation_issue`, `_jump_to_validation_issue_object` and `_format_validation_issue_line` (`app.py:16738-16752`, `:16802-16842`, `:16879-16886`) have no product caller (C lines 67-70); the S3 review recommends "Remove them and their tests. - **F-35** (= **D9-8**; **D16-6** co-located) — hidden widgets kept mounted. Reproduced (B lines 17-27): over the driven states of each window, the widgets never shown are exactly: Check Editor `#check_preview_toggle_button` (D9-8); Flow Builder `#flow_help`, `#flow_canvas`, `#flow_blocks`, `#flow_kind`, `#flow_ext_session` (D16-6 and F-35's `#flow_kind`); Flow Execution `#fx_help` (D16-6). The hidden grid canvas and numbered list are repainted on every chain change (3 blocks added → 3 paints of each, 0.33 ms a paint on the LAN host, B lines 23-24), and `#flow_kind` holds the kind the chips choose (B line 22).
- **F-51** — `esc` does not close the Reports dialog. Reproduced (B lines 29-54): `ReportViewerScreen.BINDINGS` holds `?` only and `escape` is not an active binding of the open dialog; Escape leaves the dialog open with the focus on each of its 10 focusable places (report list, report view, declared-regions box with typed text, filter dropdown, filter path box with typed text, context-bytes box with typed text, Generate, Close, `📂 Open reports/`, `?`) and on the empty dialog; with the filter dropdown open, Escape closes only the dropdown list; Escape in the help window opened from the dialog closes only the help window; Close closes the dialog. The declared-regions box (`TextArea`, `tab_behavior` `focus`) does not take Escape.
