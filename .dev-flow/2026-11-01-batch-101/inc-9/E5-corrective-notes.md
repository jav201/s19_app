- **E-6 (TC-027 in e5c-04).** The gate run in the laptop's worktree failed `test_tc027_engine_modules_unchanged_vs_main` with `['s19_app/tui/a2l.py', 's19_app/validation/rules.py']`: the test diffs against the local `main` ref, which in the laptop clone is `f9f3cb09`, an ancestor of origin/main (`git merge-base --is-ancestor` true); the two files changed on main after it (`git diff --name-only main origin/main` lists exactly them), and Inc 9's diff touches neither.
