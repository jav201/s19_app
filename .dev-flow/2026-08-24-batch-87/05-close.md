## 6 · The trial's verdict on the flow itself

**The machinery caught its own prior batch's vacuous check.** That is the batch's most useful result
about the flow, and it did not come from a design review — it came from re-running a claim at
N = 10 instead of at n = 1. batch-86 diagnosed six flaky nodes as *"every one passes in isolation"*
from a **single** isolated run per node. Re-sampled this batch, one of that very six **fails 4 of 10
alone**, and two nodes nobody had listed fail 4 of 10 and 1 of 10. The whole
"order-dependent suite pollution" classification was an artifact of stopping at the first isolated
pass. A flow that can refute its own previous batch's disposition rule, at the gate, using the rule's
own evidence, is doing the job it exists for.

**The P2 double-blocker converged.** Both independent reviewers found R2-01 — a `§5.6` fence
splicing two mutually exclusive validator runs — by **different methods** (git-state replay; the
author's own saved run files). Independent convergence on the same defect is the strongest signal a
two-lens review produces, and it is the batch-51 pattern repeating. Both blockers, and the remaining
eleven findings, were in **evidence artifacts** rather than in the substance — which is the
failure class this batch exists to eliminate, and the reason the gate forced the iteration rather
than accepting a correct conclusion resting on a spliced transcript.

**Three predictions were refuted by execution and none was overwritten.** K3's V14 arithmetic
(−2, not −1), K1's specified position (no such line exists — the block is the last thing in the
file), and the pre-registered flaky whitelist. Each was repaired by a **new measurement** beside the
wrong one, never by editing the wrong one away. The same discipline caught R-87-3b, a sixth stale-arity
site proposed by review and **refuted by arithmetic** — nine non-map screens of ten is correct, not
stale.

**What rev45 cost, and what it bought.** Cost: **3 h 11 m wall** for a spec-only batch, up from
batch-86's 1 h 15 m — and the growth is measured, not mysterious: three records edited instead of one
(forced by balancing, `LLR-87.5`), 42 outputs declared instead of 30, and a P4 that had to replace a
rule before it could sign. Bought: the last `[IFC]` census item cleared **by cause**, the pilot
contract re-authored in place, surface #3 addressable, the canon seeding debt two under its floor,
and **one flow-repo defect measured rather than suspected** (`R-87-1`: a zero-output parent silently
disables balancing for every child — found because a real BLOCK vanished, not because anyone read
the source and worried).

**The flow works. Its two standing weaknesses are both registered, not narrated:** the re-export
compounding that makes every deeper surface pay for its ancestors (`R-87-2`, must be ruled before
surface #4), and a control catalog that gained **four candidates and encoded none** — which is a
debt to the next batch, stated here so it cannot be mistaken for maturity.
