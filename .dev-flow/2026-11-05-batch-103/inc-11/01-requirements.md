## Definitions (additions and changes to CTX-A2L-FILE and CTX-A2L-TAG; terms only)

- **line end** (CTX-A2L-FILE, new): LF, CRLF or a lone CR.
- **blank** (new): space U+0020, horizontal tab U+0009, vertical tab U+000B or form feed U+000C.
- **comment** (changed): outside a quoted string, either a block comment or a line comment.
  - A block comment is the text from `/*` to the first `*/` after it. Comments do not nest.
  - A line comment is the text from `//` to the next line end.
- **quoted string** (changed): the text between a `"` and the next `"` that is not escaped. Inside it, a backslash and the character after it, when that character is not a line end, form one escaped pair, for example `\"` or `\\`. A backslash before a line end is an ordinary character, and the line end counts (E3S-12).
- **word** (new): a quoted string, or a run of word characters.
- **word character** (new): any character that is not a blank, not a line end, not `"`, and not the `/` that starts `/*` or `//`.
- **section** (changed): a block from a `/begin` word to its matching `/end` word.
  - The word after `/begin` is the section's keyword (`<NAME>`).
  - A section's **own words** are its words outside its child sections.
  - A section's **begin words** (new at the E3 correction, E3A-05; replaces the CTX *section header*, which no row uses any more) are its own words on the line of its keyword, before any child section. For a section of a named kind whose keyword line holds none of its own words, they are the own words of its first line that holds any; when those are its name alone and its next own word is a quoted string, the own words of that string's line join them (E3A-01).
- **named kind** (new, E3A-01): a section keyword whose first own word is a name: AXIS_PTS, BLOB, CHARACTERISTIC, COMPU_METHOD, COMPU_TAB, COMPU_VTAB, COMPU_VTAB_RANGE, FRAME, FUNCTION, GROUP, INSTANCE, MEASUREMENT, MEMORY_SEGMENT, MODULE, PROJECT, RECORD_LAYOUT, TRANSFORMER, every TYPEDEF_ keyword, and UNIT. Other kinds (AXIS_DESCR, MOD_COMMON, IF_DATA, ANNOTATION …) have no name word.
- **number word** (new): `0x` or `0X` followed by 1 to 16 hexadecimal digits; or 1 to 20 decimal digits. ASCII only.
- **header words** (CTX-A2L-TAG, new):
  - MEASUREMENT, 6: datatype, conversion, resolution, accuracy, lower limit, upper limit.
  - CHARACTERISTIC, 7: kind, address, deposit, maximum difference, conversion, lower limit, upper limit.
- **keyword** (CTX-A2L-TAG, new): one of the words that may stand among a MEASUREMENT's or CHARACTERISTIC's own words, with its argument count (*arity*):
  - ADDRESS_TYPE 1, ARRAY_SIZE 1, BIT_MASK 1, BYTE_ORDER 1, CALIBRATION_ACCESS 1, COMPARISON_QUANTITY 1;
  - DISCRETE 0, DISPLAY_IDENTIFIER 1, ECU_ADDRESS 1, ECU_ADDRESS_EXTENSION 1, ENCODING 1, ERROR_MASK 1;
  - EXTENDED_LIMITS 2, FORMAT 1, GUARD_RAILS 0, LAYOUT 1, MATRIX_DIM 1 to 3, MAX_REFRESH 2;
  - MODEL_LINK 1, NUMBER 1, PHYS_UNIT 1, READ_ONLY 0, READ_WRITE 0, REF_MEMORY_SEGMENT 1, STEP_SIZE 1, SYMBOL_LINK 2;
  - and the tool extensions the reader reads today: DATA_SIZE 1, LENGTH 1, DATA_TYPE 1, FUNCTION 1, COMPU_METHOD 1, FORMULA 1, UNIT 1, LOWER_LIMIT 1, UPPER_LIMIT 1, VIRTUAL 0, CALIBRATABLE 0, DEPOSIT 1.
  - A keyword is an unquoted word; a quoted word is never a keyword. A keyword's **argument words** are the words that follow it among the tag's own words, up to the next keyword, the place of a child section or the end of the own words: the first *arity* of them, and for MATRIX_DIM all of them. A keyword is never an argument word (E3S-02). A **surplus word** of an ECU_ADDRESS, DATA_SIZE, LENGTH, NUMBER, ARRAY_SIZE or BIT_MASK keyword is a *hex-like* word that follows its argument word before the next keyword, the place of a child section or the end of the own words: an unquoted word that is a number word, starts with a decimal digit, or is made only of the ASCII hex digits and `_`, such as `0x3E00`, `3E00`, `2D3E00`, `E00`, `_4026` or `F0` (E3S-24; round 2 took number words only, widened at the round-3 addenda as ruled). Any other word there, such as the identifier `SIG_X`, is dropped, as base (corpus: 9 words after an ECU_ADDRESS argument, all identifiers, none hex-like; E3S-24 [CORP3]).
  - A keyword **gives no number** when it has fewer argument words than its arity, when one of its argument words is not a number word, when it has a surplus word, or, for MATRIX_DIM, when it has more than 3 argument words.
- **MATRIX_DIM** (CTX-A2L-TAG, changed): the product of the 1 to 3 numbers after the MATRIX_DIM keyword; 1 when the tag has no MATRIX_DIM keyword (E3S-08). Rows R-A2L-014 and R-A2L-016 cite it.
- **address words** (CTX-A2L-TAG, new, E3S-03): a CHARACTERISTIC's header address (its second header word) and the argument word of each ECU_ADDRESS keyword of a tag.
- **byte-order word** and **byte-order source** (CTX-A2L-TAG, new at the round-3 addenda, E3S-27): a *byte-order word* is MSB_FIRST, MSB_LAST, BIG_ENDIAN or LITTLE_ENDIAN in any letter case, as base reads them (`_normalize_byte_order`, `a2l.py:302-308`); a quoted word is none. A tag's *byte-order source* is the BYTE_ORDER argument word its effective byte order comes from: its own BYTE_ORDER keyword's; when it has none, the first BYTE_ORDER word of its record layout; when that has none, the first of its module's MOD_COMMON. With none of the three, it has no source and takes `little`, as base.
- **sizes** (CTX-A2L-TAG, new, E3S-03, E3A-13; grouped at the round-2 correction, E3S-19, E3S-23): the sizes a tag declares, in two groups. Its **byte sizes** are the number after each DATA_SIZE and LENGTH keyword. Its **counts** are the number after each NUMBER and ARRAY_SIZE keyword and the product of its MATRIX_DIM numbers when it has a MATRIX_DIM keyword; a tag with a DATA_SIZE or LENGTH keyword and none of these has the one count 1. The byte sizes are always compared with each other, and the counts with each other. When the datatype's size is known, each count × that size is compared with the byte sizes too, and the sizes are written in bytes; otherwise counts are written as elements. For a CURVE, MAP, CUBOID, CUBE_4 or CUBE_5, the counts are not compared, and its byte sizes are compared with its layout span when that span is known: from inline axes in the walk, or from external axes in the external-axis pass (E3S-29, round-3 addenda). A tag's sizes **agree** when every compared pair is equal, or when it has nothing to compare.
- **length rows** (new, E3A-13 (1)): R-A2L-013 … 017, R-A2L-READ.53 and .57 give a length only to a tag whose sizes agree and none of whose size keywords gives no number; R-A2L-READ.61 gives every other tag no length. That refusal is final: no layout-span step, inline-axis step or external-axis pass gives the tag a length afterwards (E3S-23). A tag with no length has no byte size in its enriched row either (`enrich_a2l_tags_with_values`), so no value is decoded for it and no picker sizes it from its layout (E3S-18, round 2). In R-A2L-010 … 036, a keyword "line" reads as the keyword with its argument words (R-A2L-READ.29; E3A-13 (5)).
- **datatype (CHARACTERISTIC)** (CTX-A2L-TAG, new at the E1 addendum, CG-2): the datatype of the FNC_VALUES of the CHARACTERISTIC's record layout; when the layout names none, the datatype its deposit name spells out (as R-A2L-022 and R-A2L-023 take it). Rows R-A2L-015, R-A2L-016 and R-A2L-READ.53 cite it.
- **declared span** (CTX-A2L-TAG, new): an INSTANCE or BLOB of the A2L file, given as its name, its kind (`INSTANCE` or `BLOB`), its address, and a length or no length. Declared spans are not tags.
- **reference reading** (new): the reading of an A2L file under these definitions and the rows of S2.1 and S2.2, by a lexer written for the tests that does not import the reader. It is the oracle of R-A2L-READ.48.
- **hostile corpus** (new): the shapes listed under "Common fixtures" in `02-test-cases.md`: at least 60 shapes, every class this file names.
- **defect tags at e07fb00** (new): the tags listed at the end of this file.
