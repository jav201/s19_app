## User stories

- **US-103-05** — "The Patch Editor edits a parameter by its A2L definition: scalars, enums, bit masks, text, curves, maps and value blocks." Inc 9b adds what the operator requires of the bench's long jobs (P-127): while the bench decodes, previews or stages a large object, the staging sheet shows that the window is working — an amber border and a BUSY pill with a moving spinner, the job, the object and the elapsed seconds — and it stays live through the record-stage write, so the user does not force the application closed.

## Scope

- **In:** the busy cue (border and pill) for the selected bench row's decode and preview and for every record stage; the cue's tick; the cue leaving on every way a job ends; a record stage's write and view refresh run in steps that let the event loop turn between them (O-E), so the cue and the keys stay live through it; the pill's hostile-name rules.
- **Out:** the operator's measured key→job gap is diagnosed at E4, not fixed by a row here (`E1-slice.md` §5; the cue rows bound what the user sees whatever the cause); R-PE-BENCH.29 and .82 are not changed (`E1-slice.md` §6: E4 measures whether O-E brings .29/2 and .82/1 back under 0.5 s; the proposal goes to the operator); cancelling a running I-2 decode or encode (a thread cannot be stopped: backlog BL-9b-1, O-A); a stepped refresh for Undo, Redo, Apply and the other five refresh sites (backlog BL-9b-2); a cue for jobs outside the bench (file load, execute); a progress count (I-2 gives none, `E1-rulings.md` VP-1); the sheet's own layout, colours and widths (Inc 8b).

## Definitions (added to CTX-PE-BENCH — terms and formats only)

```text
busy job        A record stage, from the alt+s that asks for it until its document write and its view
                refresh end (asked, queued, encoding, writing or refreshing); else a decode or a preview
                of the selected bench row while the row's bench state names that job (asked, queued or
                running). At most one busy job is shown: a record stage when one exists, else the
                selected row's job. Job word: `stage` (record stage), `decode`, `preview`.
                A page row's summary decode and another row's staged decode are not busy jobs.
pending time    The seconds since the busy job was asked: the handling of the key (or the selection)
                that asked for it, kept while the same job word and name stay the busy job.
cue tick        A UI-thread timer of 0.1 s that runs while a busy job exists. The spinner advances one
                frame at each tick that runs.
busy colour     #f5c26b, the colour of the bench's edit style (Inc 9 C9 D-6; hue census entry
                `definition_bench_view.EDIT`). No new colour.
busy border     The staging sheet window's border drawn `round` in the busy colour.
busy pill       `` <f> BUSY · <job word> <name> · <s> s · keys still work `` — one space before <f> and one
                after `work` — in bold, the app background colour (#0a0e1b, insight_style.DEPTH_BG) on
                the busy colour; <f> the spinner frame; <s> the pending time, one decimal (`0.3`, `12.0`);
                <name> the busy name.
spinner frames  `◐` `◓` `◑` `◒`, in that order, then again.
busy title      The window's border title while the cue shows: `STAGING SHEET`, two spaces, the busy pill.
busy name       The status echo of the job's row name (CTX-PE-BENCH), cut so the busy title fits the
                window's top border: at most max(1, B − F) characters, the last of them `…` when cut;
                B = the characters the top border shows for its title, F = the busy title's length
                without the name. A title still wider than B is cropped at its right end by the border.
busy cue        The busy border and the busy title together.
idle look       The window's border and title with no busy cue: the border of the stylesheet (`round`,
                the rule colour; the accent while the focus is within) and the title `STAGING SHEET`.
grace           0.2 s of pending time: a busy job shows its cue only once its pending time reaches it.
write and       The UI-thread part of a record stage after its encode: the guarded write (R-PE-BENCH.70
refresh         … .83) and the refresh of the ledger, the JSON view, the staging sheet and the checks.
quiet host      `<runner>`, 1-minute load average under 1.0 read by `uptime` beside each timed result
                (HT-08). A run on a loaded host is a record, not a verdict.
```

## Requirements

Threshold = the number of independently asserted cases that must pass (`n/n`). Negative control = a mutant of the product that must turn the case's test RED (run on a copy of the tree, HT-09). Examples (`EX-BC*`) are in `codewire/graph-inc9b.md`. LARGE = Inc 9's hand A2L VAL_BLK `UBYTE`, `MATRIX_DIM 1048576`, `NO_COMPU_METHOD` (decode ≈ 10–11 s, `../inc-3/evidence-e3/sec-bench-transcript.txt`); DEFC = Inc 9's `tools/synth/make_release_case.py --case definitions` rows. A "gate" is the test's hold on a job (Inc 9's existing held-job fixture), released by the test.
