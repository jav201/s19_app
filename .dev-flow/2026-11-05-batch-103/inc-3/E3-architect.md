## Verdict: FAIL

1 MAJOR, 2 MEDIUM, 7 MINOR, 9 LOW, 4 INFO; no HIGH, no CRITICAL. The 3a design is sound, fits Inc 2a's I-2 without changing what 2a defines (§3), keeps every lookup in `describe_definitions`'s one walk (§2), and states every ASAM-text decision as a rule the system applies (§2). It is close to the smallest design for its rows. One defect blocks E4, and its fix makes the design smaller:
- **E3A-01.** `_read_axis` builds every FIX_AXIS's stored points at describe time, up to 1,048,576 per axis, and the definition keeps them, even on a refused one. One 5-line CURVE costs 0.66 s and 42 MB [Q5]; four cost 2.6 s and 168 MB. A crafted A2L of a few hundred lines exhausts memory when Inc 6 describes its rows. That breaks R-A2L-DEF.58.2 (an exception in place of a refusal), and it is 3a's own design, not the reader's. Fix: build FIX points at decode only.

Two MEDIUM findings are record gaps on the PE-D1 path Inc 6 consumes:
- **E3A-02.** No 3a case fails on 2a's `_object_kind`: a leading-1 MATRIX_DIM (512 public objects) stays a scalar.
- **E3A-03.** E2-11's "element count 0 = unknown" has no row and contradicts R-A2L-DEF.73.
