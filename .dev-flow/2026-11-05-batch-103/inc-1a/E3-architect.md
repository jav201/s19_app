- D-1 A, D-3 A: the Tk read and Tk write go, with the singleton, the priming, `atexit` and the constants. That removes C-D1 (3.4 s) and C-D2 (the dead Tk write) in the two slice files.

- The test arrangement holds: the guard comes before every write, the probe fails loud while dependents skip, and the job lives in a separate workflow. The extra work is mostly in placement and controls.

**What blocks:**
- **I-1 cannot be used by its consumer.** Inc 1b's ruled seam and its app.py-only file budget conflict with the pinned copy tail (E3A-01).

- **Some timing rows have no negative control that reliably goes RED.** The PowerShell-first control does not on CI, and the copy rows have none at all on any runnable platform (E3A-03).
