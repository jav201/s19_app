## User story

### US-103-01 — copy and paste work in every window (the copy-out half)
Commission (PLAN.md): "Text copied from any part of the TUI can be pasted into any text field of the TUI and into other Windows programs, and a paste lands at once." Operator note of 2026-11-05: "escenarios de copiar texto desde varias partes de la TUI y pegado a las ventanas de texto disponibles en la TUI". Inc 1b's part (re-cut by the E1 addendum, Operator rulings 2): as the operator, I want a right-click on the text I selected in a view, or on a tag row of a table, to copy it into the Windows clipboard — and Ctrl+C to copy what I selected — so that I can paste it into a text box of any window and into other programs; and I want Ctrl+C with nothing selected to ask before the app quits, as the terminal's quit key. Inc 1a owns the text boxes' own copy, cut and paste (and their right-click).

## Definitions (new context note CTX-COPY, S7, beside CTX-PASTE; terms and formats only)

Inc 1a's CTX-PASTE terms are used as it defines them: *OS clipboard* (the Windows clipboard; Inc 1a D-2), *app clipboard*, *text box*, *selection* (in a text box), *write failure warning*, *OSC 52 clipboard request*, *responsive*.

```text
copy key        Ctrl+C.
view            Any part of a window or dialog outside a text box that shows text: a pane,
                a line, a label, a card, a list, a table.
view selection  Text the operator marks in a view: by a mouse drag, or in the JSON view also
                by Shift+arrows or F7.
right-click     A press and a release of the right mouse button at the same screen_offset,
                on the same screen and widget, with no intervening MouseMove whose
                screen_offset differs from the press, that reach the app: the terminal
                reports the mouse while the app asks for mouse reports (WT-doc: "If an
                application is in mouse mode, you can hold down Shift to make a selection
                instead of sending VT input"); a Shift+right-click and a Shift+drag stay
                the terminal's own.                              [addendum; E3-01]
right-drag      A right-button press followed before release by a MouseMove whose
                screen_offset differs from the press. Leaving and returning to the press
                cell is still a right-drag, not a right-click.                 [E3-01]
table row       A row of the A2L tag table or of the MAC table, not its header; its cell
                metadata has out_of_bounds=False. A valid row index with
                out_of_bounds=True is empty space, not a table row.       [addendum, E3A-02]
empty right-click  A right-click outside a text box, neither on a view that holds part of
                a view selection nor on a table row; out_of_bounds=True padding is empty
                space even when its row index is valid.                    [addendum, A-3, E3A-02]
nothing selected  No non-empty view selection on the top screen (in the JSON view: none
                while it has the focus) and no selection in the focused text box. A
                materialized screen selection whose extracted text is "" counts as nothing
                selected.                                                [addendum, A-2, E3A-01]
quit dialog     Title "Quit?". Text "Quit the app? Nothing is selected to copy."
                Buttons "Quit (y)" and "Stay (n)"; "Stay (n)" is the emphasised button.
                [addendum, A-9; G2 r2 q3_body=B]
  Quit          y, or Enter or a click on "Quit (y)".
  Stay          n, Escape, or Enter or a click on "Stay (n)".
text view       (withdrawn with R-COPY.3 by the addendum; kept for that row's history)
                A view that takes the focus and shows lines of text:
                  main hex view, A2L hex, MAC hex (S4 CTX-HEX; the focus is on the pane that
                    holds the rows)
                  A2L summary (Workspace, right pane, below Coverage Stats)
                  JSON view (Patch Editor, JSON tab, the read-only view, S10 CTX-PE-JSON)
table           The A2L tag table (S2 CTX-A2L-SCREEN) and the MAC table (S2 CTX-MAC).   [D-4]
highlighted row As CTX-A2L-SCREEN: the row under the table cursor.
shown           As CTX-SHELL's "shown widget" (b101 Inc 12 / 12b; whichever merges first
                folds it): displayed, with every widget that holds it, up to its window.
shown text      (withdrawn with R-COPY.3 by the addendum)
                The text a view paints, as the operator reads it: every painted line in order,
                its marker column, notice lines and blank cells included, the lines joined
                by a line feed, no line feed after the last line.
row name        The full tag name associated with a table row, without its leading glyph,
                a tab or an internal row key; never a shortened painted cell
                that follows it: the tag name (A2L tag table), the record name (MAC table).
                Markup and representable C0 characters (including ESC, BEL and LF) are kept
                verbatim; U+0000 is governed by R-COPY.25, not replaced or sanitized.
                [addendum: of the right-clicked row, A-1; was the highlighted row's; E3-02/03]
copied text     The text a copy takes: a view selection, a row name, or a text box's
                selection (Inc 1a's copy and cut, which reach the same copy).   [addendum]
both clipboards The OS clipboard and the app clipboard.
copy limit      65,536 characters (the paste limit of CTX-PASTE).
capped payload  The copied text's first 65,536 characters, or all of it when shorter.
                The original length is retained for the cut notice.                [E3-02]
copy notice     "Copied 1 line to the clipboard." when the copied text has one line, else
                "Copied <n> lines to the clipboard.", <n> its number of lines: the line
                feeds it holds plus one, a line feed at its very end not counted; an
                information notice. One for every copy, text boxes included (I-1). [G2]
cut notice      "Copied the first 65,536 characters to the clipboard (<N> in all).", <N> the
                copied text's length in characters, written with a comma every three digits;
                an information notice.
NUL refusal warning  "Clipboard copy refused — NUL cannot be represented in the Windows text
                clipboard."; one warning notice, 6 s. Its fixed WARNING log line is
                "OS clipboard copy refused; payload contains NUL", without payload or a
                payload prefix.                                                     [E3-02]
nothing notice  (withdrawn with R-COPY.5 by the addendum: a bare Ctrl+C opens the quit
                dialog; an empty right-click shows nothing)
                "Nothing copied — select text with the mouse first."; an information notice.
terminal copy   A copy the terminal makes from its own selection (Shift+drag in Windows
                Terminal); it never reaches the app.
```
