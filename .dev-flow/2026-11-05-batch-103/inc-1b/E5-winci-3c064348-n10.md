## What the remaining failure points to

R-PASTE.13/101 is a missing-notice observation, not a wrong notice. Two readings, neither confirmed from the record:
1. The failure notice is posted after `ledger.tail_done` is set, and `_observe` runs before the notice reaches the app's notification list (an ordering race in the test, not in the product).
2. The `_wait` condition (`writer_done and tail_done`) was met, but the notice path was skipped or suppressed in that run (a product-side intermittent).
