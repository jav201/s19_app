# E5 WinCI N=10 on 7804b500 (P-001 F-1 fix, option 2)

## Verdict

Option 2 (sleep-poll wait loop + `gc.collect()`/`gc.freeze()` before the gesture) held the 0.200 s bound on every gated node: R-PASTE.14/101, /102, /103 and C1[key|right|row] passed in 10 of 10 runs (0/10 failures each). Round 3 and round 4 each failed 2 to 3 of these nodes in a single run.

The only R-PASTE.14 node that failed is the control C2 (`freeze_gc=False` by design): 7 of 10 runs. Its diagnostics show the same cause as round 4: one gen-2 collection of 0.172-0.406 s inside the window. That is the control working, not a regression of the fix.

One failure is not a timing node: run 43516439297 failed `test_r_paste_13_101_held_row_copy_warns_within_bound` (R-PASTE.13, write-failure notice sequence), obligations 107/108. One occurrence in 10; cause not established from the log (see "Not R-PASTE.14 timing").

Base for all runs: 7804b500, branch b103-inc1b, repo jav201/s19_app. Run 43516361213 is the push run; the other nine are re-runs. Host load was not controlled (HT-08): runner wall time varied from 28 min to 44 min.

## Gated nodes: GC evidence

With `gc.freeze()` active, the six gated nodes never failed, so no gated window produced a stack capture. In the QC-TM margin tables of the failing runs, /101, /102, /103 and C1 sit at 0.029-0.060 s of 0.200 s (70 to 86 % margin); in the three green runs they sit at 0.013-0.106 s (47 to 93 %). The worst gated value seen in the 10 runs is 0.106 s (/102, run 43516422099), 53 % of the bound. A gen-2 collection of 0.17-0.41 s, as seen in C2, would break the bound; freezing removed it from the gated windows.

## Not R-PASTE.14 timing

Run 43516439297, `test_r_paste_13_101_held_row_copy_warns_within_bound`: `AssertionError: write-failure notice sequence differed` at `_failure` (warnings != [(WRITE_FAIL, "warning", 6.0)]). Captured log: `write_os_clipboard: every strategy failed (cascade=['win32', 'powershell'])`. The held-clipboard cascade ran; the notice list differed from the expected single 6.0 s warning. Gate: obligations 107/108, exit 1. The log does not show the observed notice list, so the cause (a duplicate notice, a missing notice, or a different timeout) is not established. It is also the one run with C2 max lateness 0.232 s (the lowest) and a 31 min runtime. No P-002 teardown node failed in any run; artifact scan and gate obligations were 108/108 in the other nine.

## What the remaining failures point to

- The C2 failures are the intended control: with `freeze_gc=False` a gen-2 GC (heap built by the Textual app and the test) lands in the window in 7 of 10 runs. This confirms the round-4 finding on a larger N and confirms that the freeze, not the sleep-poll alone, is what clears /101-/103 and C1.
- The R-PASTE.13/101 failure is a different mechanism (notice sequence, not time) and is a single occurrence; N = 10 gives no rate better than 1/10 with wide uncertainty.
