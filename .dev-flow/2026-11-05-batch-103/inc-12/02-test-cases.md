## Common fixtures

- **SYNMAP** (from Inc 11) — the synthetic MAP of `../inc-11/evidence-e1/e1-pilot-explorer-widths.py`: `SYN_MAP_W` at 0x1C2D3E00, on `RL_SYN_MAP` (NO_AXIS_PTS_X, NO_AXIS_PTS_Y, FNC_VALUES UWORD); two FIX_AXIS axes of 87 and 13 points, length 2266; an S19 image holding its 2266 bytes.
- **LONGMAP(n)** (from Inc 11) — SYNMAP with its name replaced by an `n`-character name `SYN_MAP_` + `L` × (`n` − 12) + `_END`. Only 32-bit addresses are on its page, so the Address column is 12 cells and the Length column 8 (the label `Length` is wider than `2266`).
- **PAGE64(n)** (new) — the page of `../inc-11/evidence-e3/corr2-e3c2-fixedcols.py` (`a2l_text(n)`): the MAP `SYN_MAP_…` at 0x1C2D3E00 (length 2266), the MEASUREMENT `SYN_V64_…` at `0x1C2D3E004026` (UWORD; above 0xFFFFFFFF, kept by R-VAL-036) and the MEASUREMENT `SYN_BIG_…` at 0x00001000 with `LENGTH 1048576`; every name `n` characters (`name_of(prefix, n)`); the same image. The Address column is 16 cells and the Length column 9.
- **PAGE64W(n)** (new) — PAGE64(n) with one more MEASUREMENT `SYN_W16_…` at `0xFEDCBA9876543210` (16 hexadecimal digits, the widest address Inc 11's R-A2L-READ.69 lets a tag keep; corrected at E3, E3A-07: a 20-digit decimal number word reads above it). The Address column is 20 cells.
- **MANY(k, n)** (new at E3, E3A-02) — the probe's `page_many` (`codewire/evidence-e2/i12e2-probe.py:129-136`): LONGMAP(n)'s MAP plus k MEASUREMENTs `SYN_M<iii>_…` of n characters at the 32-bit addresses 0x2000 + 2i; optionally MEASUREMENT i at `0x1C2D3E004026`. Pages of 200 rows (`a2l_tags_page_size`); `plus` and `minus` change the page.
- **Real Explorer** (from Inc 11) — the app started headless (`tests/support/b100_projects.run_app`) with the size of the case. The flow is: `load_selected_file(image)`, `load_a2l_from_path(a2l)`, then the workers drain. Key `2` shows the A2L Explorer, and the cursor goes on row 0. The painted lines are read back from `render_line(y).text` of `#a2l_tags_list` and `#a2l_detail_card`.
- **Cell spans and *w*** (new) — a cell's span is read as F reads it (`cell_report` in `corr2-e3c2-fixedcols.py`): column widths from `column.get_render_width(table)`, in `table.ordered_columns` order; the visible width from `table.scrollable_content_region.width`; fixed columns at their own x, other columns shifted by `table.scroll_x`. The width left and *w* are computed from these values (`01-requirements.md` Definitions), never from the case's terminal width. F's layout (height 54, 3 rows, no vertical scrollbar) gives visible widths of 38, 41, 44, 46, 49, 60, 73 and 108 cells at 80, 85, 90, 95, 100, 118, 160 and 220 columns [F A N… W…]; the expected values below use them, and a case whose measured visible width differs recomputes them from the formula of R-TUI-042a.5.
- **Scrolled fully right** — `table.scroll_to(x=table.max_scroll_x, animate=False)`, then the pilot pauses (F).
