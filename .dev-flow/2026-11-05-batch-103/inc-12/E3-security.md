## Questions routed to E3 — answers

- **E2-7 (shortened names that look alike, the wrong row for "Use parameter" or a copy):** E3S-03. The problem is broader than E2-7 says. "Use parameter" does not read this table. A copy takes the cursor row's full name. The Address (whole, first column) and the card tell rows apart, and the card can do that safely once E3S-02 is fixed. The operator rules on it in AT-B103-I12-2.
- **Can a painted cell still mislead about the address or length?** Not in the table, on the measured pages:
  - [H] at 118 and 80 columns: every Address and Length cell painted whole on the design (5/5 rows each) and 0/5 on base;
  - [F] after floods: 28/28, 34/34 and 28/28;
  - the address bound: E3S-05.
  - The card is the open path: E3S-02.
- **Markup and control characters; Inc 2's U+FFFD rule:**
  - The Tag cell deletes C0 and C1 characters (`safe_text`). That merges `A<ESC>B` with `AB`, but READ.4 records an error for both.
  - The cell keeps the Cf, Zl, Zp and Mn categories [U char].
  - Markup stays literal: the cut works on plain text and `safe_text` never parses markup.
  - The U+FFFD rule should reach the Tag cell: E3S-01.
- **Bidirectional controls:** E3S-04. The order change removes the base exposure of the Address.
- **Very long or zero-width names against the width computation:** no name pushes Address out. A 1,000,000-character name is linear and cheap. Zero-width runs end inside the visible width per rich [H row4]. The break is cost: E3S-01. Any rich-vs-terminal width mismatch can only move cells after the Tag cell.
- **The resize-driven rebuild under a flood:** E3S-07.
